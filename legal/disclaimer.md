## Appraisal and Valuation Disclaimer

The Platform does not provide or relay dealer offers or appraisals. Calculator results and educational content are estimates, not statements of market value or guaranteed sale prices. Independent dealer opinions may be subjective, based on limited information, and subject to change after physical inspection.

We strongly recommend:

1. **Getting multiple independent opinions** — ask whether an evaluation has a fee before proceeding
2. **Not relying solely on online appraisals** for high-value items
3. **Having valuable items professionally graded** by PCGS, NGC, or other recognized grading services before selling
4. **Researching current market values** independently before accepting any offer

- Certification of dealer expertise or trustworthiness
- A guarantee of dealer conduct or business practices

`Claimed` means a representative relationship passed manual review. `Verified` means displayed public business details were independently checked. Neither state is affected by payment or promotion.

We encourage visitors to:

- Research Dealers independently before transacting
- Check for membership in recognized organizations (ANA, PNG, state numismatic associations)

- Meet in safe, public locations for transactions

## Melt Value Calculator Disclaimer

The melt value calculator provided on the Platform:

- Uses displayed spot-price data when available and allows user-entered adjustments; pricing may be delayed or inaccurate
- Calculates approximate melt value based on standard coin specifications
- Does **not** account for numismatic (collector) premiums, rarity, condition, or errors
- Should **not** be used as the sole basis for any financial decision

Actual coin values may differ significantly from melt value. Consult a qualified numismatic professional for accurate valuations.
