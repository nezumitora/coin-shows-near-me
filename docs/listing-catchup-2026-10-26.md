## Backlog evidence, not a missing-show count

The October 25 scheduled comparison artifact (run `40198661144`, comparison ID
`08728e35-d098-441f-bc3a-9d6866ebef4c`) reports 197 canonical listings before
this batch, 78 in the explicit registry, 71 approved-source listings waiting for
reviewed comparison batches, and 48 without an approved comparison source.
These categories describe coverage; they must not be summed into a count of
missing shows or presumed changes. Some sources return redirects, 403/404 or
connection failures, and some calendar contents require JavaScript or image review.

The October 25 discovery run `40199666789` contains third-party leads, not
publishable facts. Dates appearing elsewhere on a source page must never be
assigned to a nearby show without association evidence. In particular, the
Michigan calendar's November 14 and December 5 dates belong to other shows,
not New Baltimore and Southfield respectively.
