# Directory expert follow-up questions

## Current verification rule

A form submission does not establish verification by itself. Verification requires independently checked evidence from an official organizer or promoter website, a confirmed organizer representative, an official club or association page, an official venue calendar or representative, or a clearly official organizer/club/venue social account.

Use distinct provenance labels:

- `Organizer verified`: an organizer, promoter, club officer, or authorized show staff member whose role was independently checked.
- `Official source verified`: listing facts matched an official published source.
- `Venue confirmed`: an official venue source or checked venue representative confirmed the event.
- `Dealer reported`: a participating dealer supplied information that still requires independent review.
- `Community reported`: an attendee or collector supplied information that still requires independent review.

Dealer and community reports may trigger research or corroborate other evidence, but they do not create a verified status by themselves.

## Questions for the expert

1. What evidence is sufficient before labeling a listing `Organizer verified`? Is a response from an email address or social account published on the official organizer website sufficient?
2. If a show has no official website, can an official venue calendar, coin club, numismatic association, or checked venue representative establish verification?
3. Should organizer verification expire after a fixed interval, such as six or twelve months, or when the confirmed event date passes?
4. Should public provenance distinguish `Organizer verified`, `Official source verified`, and `Venue confirmed`, while treating dealer and attendee submissions as reports until independently checked?
5. Should anyone be able to submit confirmations and corrections for manual review, or should confirmation be restricted to organizers and official representatives?
6. For a recurring show awaiting its next date, should its card disappear from state and city listings or appear in a separate `Recurring — next date pending` section while its permanent page remains indexed?
7. For a one-time event page with useful information or backlinks but no future occurrence, should it remain indexed as historical content, receive `noindex`, or be improved into a historical recap?
8. Should a thin retired page spend any time as `noindex` before returning a genuine 404 or 410?
9. When a recurring page has no confirmed next date, should Event structured data be removed completely until a new date is verified?
10. Which founding-dealer benefit should be tested first: featured placement, show-attendance listings, enhanced profiles, or qualified seller inquiries?

## Already-recorded guidance

Do not re-ask whether unrelated expired pages should redirect to the homepage; they should not. Keep one stable indexed URL for credible recurring series, update it when new dates are confirmed, remove passed dates from upcoming sections, retain useful date history, redirect only to an exact replacement, and use a genuine 404/410 for thin one-time pages with no useful history or successor.
